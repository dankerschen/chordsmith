Install Fretwise

On iPhone or iPad, in Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

Fretwise then opens from its own icon, full screen, and works without Wi-Fi.
